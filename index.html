Before you begin

A note on compatibility.

Fleu requires iOS 26 or later on an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone, with Apple Intelligence enabled and its on-device model ready.

Saving an entry currently requires successful mood analysis. If the model is unavailable, an entry cannot be saved.

Mood estimates are for personal reflection. They can be wrong and are not medical advice or a diagnosis.
